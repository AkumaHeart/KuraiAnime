魔法少女リリカルなのは (Magical Girl Lyrical Nanoha)

Genres: RomanceAnimeMahō ShōjoAction

Nanoha Takamachi is a third grade school student. She lives with her parents, who own a coffee shop, her sister, and her brother, who are in high school and college, respectively. Nanoha´s normal life changes when she hears a voice calling her back home and she ends up rescuing an injured ferret (whose human form is a boy archaeologist, Yūno, who hails from another world). From him she obtains a magical crystal called Raising Heart (レイジングハート, Reijingu Hāto), which gives Nanoha magical powers. After their meeting, Nanoha and Yūno must obtain the 21 "Jewel Seeds" (ジュエルシード, Jueru Shīdo), powerful crystals from Yūno´s world that fulfill wishes and are dangerous if they fall into the wrong hands. However, Nanoha and Yūno are not the only ones after the Jewel Seeds.
