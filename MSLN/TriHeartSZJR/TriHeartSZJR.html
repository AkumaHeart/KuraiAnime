とらいあんぐるハート さざなみ女子寮 (Triangle Heart: Sazanami Joshiryō)

Genres: YuriHentaiMahō ShōjoAction

Warning: Explicit content

Kosuke Makihara works as a substitute janitor of a huge boarding house called Sazanami Joshiryō mostly consisting of young girls from grades of elementary school through high school, while the regular janitor and the dorms owner, who is his brother in law Keigo and his aunt Kana respectively, are traveling abroad in Hong Kong, China. The story follows the lives of Kousuke and the girls and introduces each them one by one.
